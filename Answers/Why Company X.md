## Afresh

Source: [[Afresh]]

> Why Afresh? Tell us what makes you excited to join our team.

```
Afresh's mission, to tackle food waste, is what excites me most about Afresh. Believe it or not, this is a problem I think about all the time at my house (ask my wife!).  Moreover, you guys are doing it in a way that actually works for grocers. That's something I can get on board with!

On the technical side, the Ordering role lines up with where I've spent my career. I've built distributed, event-driven backend systems for over a decade now. At Octimine, for example, I replaced a 48-hour weekly batch job with a real-time Kafka pipeline.

I'm also excited about the ownership and mentorship side of this role. I've spent the past few years running my own company, and I'm ready to get back on a team. I do my best work and learn the most in collaboration, and I'd love to help guide junior engineers while growing into more technical leadership myself.

And "billions of decisions from the store floor to the distribution center" sounds like a really fun domain to dig into!
```
