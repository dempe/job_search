> Have you previously supported a SaaS company or a custom software application?

```
Yes to both.

I've spent the majority of my career at SaaS analytics companies. At DataRank (YC W13), I built the data ingestion that collected what people were saying about our customers' products. At SimplyMeasured/Sprout Social, I built APIs and microservices in Go and Spring Boot and set up the API gateway in front of them, giving customers data about their social media campaigns. At Octimine, I replaced a fragile weekly batch job with a real-time Kafka Streams pipeline processing ~30 million patent documents weekly.

As for custom software, as a contractor at Dekstech, building and supporting custom apps is my primary role. For example, I built a Node.js payment integration between Shopify and a payment provider, a Shopify-embedded Node.js/React app for a B2B coffee distributor, and an internal app we use to estimate projects and pay our engineers.
```
