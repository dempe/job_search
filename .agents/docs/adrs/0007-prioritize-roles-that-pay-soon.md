# 0007: Prioritize roles that pay soon

## Context

The user needs income urgently. Getting paid soon matters more than the ideal role, salary range, or mission fit. A job that pays below their usual range can still be the right move as a stopgap while the search continues.

## Decision

Job assessments weigh how quickly a role would start paying. Fast-moving roles come first: contract and contract-to-hire work, immediate starts, recruiters who respond quickly, and short hiring processes. Long processes with many rounds and take-home challenges rank lower.

## How to apply

- In `/djd`, the assessment notes how fast the role would start paying, and the verdict mentions it.
- Don't advise against applying only because the pay is low. State the pay plainly and let the user decide.
- When suggesting where to look, favor contract and quick-hire channels.
