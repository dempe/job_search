### Questions to Ask

- Is there a contract to sign?
- How do you currently handle deployments? Is infra managed manually or through IaC?
- What's the timeline?
- Is this project paid upon completion of the entire engagement, or upon completion of specific milestones?
- Just to make sure expectations match — will I be paid directly on fixed terms, or does payment depend on when your client pays you?
- Who pays me — you or the client?
- What is the approval process for deliverables? Who signs off and how fast do approvals happen?
- Do you have access to the current AWS account architecture diagram, or is part of the job reconstructing it?
	- If you have to reconstruct the system blind, that's not a $25–$60/hr project. That's a consultant role.
- What are the symptoms of what you're experiencing?
	- Timeouts?
	- Errors?
	- Slow processing?
- What caused the last failure or incident? What was the recovery process?
	- This tells us if they
		- Understand their own system
		- Collect metrics
		- Expect me to be the firefighter for ongoing chaos
- What is the success story for this project? If it goes perfectly, what will change in your system?
	- This is a Rorschach test. If they answer in vague terms ("it'll run faster"), that means they don't actually know what the problem is. If they answer with specifics ("job queue latency under 500 ms; eliminate timeouts; reduce EC2 CPU spikes"), they have a real plan.
- What's your current tech stack?
- What do you have in terms of monitoring and logging?
- What CloudWatch dashboards do you have?
- How would you define accomplished for this project?
	- Metrics?
	- Are there any SLOs or SLAs?
- How are the PHP workers deployed?
	- Cron?
	- Supervisord?
	- Docker?
	- Laravel Horizon?
- Do you have any DLQs?
- What skills does the current dev team have, and where exactly is the bottleneck?
