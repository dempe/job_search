The annotations are how you tell Spring what to manage and how to wire it together.

On startup, @SpringBootApplication triggers a component scan of the package tree. Anything marked @Component, @Service, @Repository, or @Controller gets registered as a bean definition in the application context. @Configuration classes contribute beans explicitly through @Bean methods.
