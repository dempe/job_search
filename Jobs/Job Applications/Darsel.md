## Application

A good approach for the YC jobs website is to pick a few of their problems and ask for more details on them while following up with my own experience.

```
Hi!

I'm Chris Dempewolf. I love the idea of Darsel. I'm currently working in math education myself! I built Athena Math, a mental math trainer and am preparing to release Hermes Count, a subitization trainer for building number sense.

I'm curious to learn a little more about the problems you're facing at Darsel.

1. You mentioned optimizing MySQL configurations and access patterns. I'm curious - what's your current bottleneck - query performance, connection pooling, or something else? At Dennemeyer/Octimine, I had to optimize both of these on our Postgres backend.

2. Backend functionality around data retrieval and analysis -- are you building from scratch or do you have something in place already? I've spent pretty much my entire career in data ingestion/orchestration, building pipelines and providing analytics. Are you microservices-based or monolithic?

3. 10-100x growth over the next year - what's the current request volume, and where do you anticipate the first scaling bottlenecks? I've built serverless systems with Lambda and DynamoDB specifically for scale, but I'm wondering if you're more concerned about database scaling, Lambda cold starts, or something else? Since you're on Lambda, containerization to ECS/EKS could be a smart move, but depends on your circumstances.

Anyways, it sounds like you've got some interesting problems to solve and it's a great mission. Hope to hear from you soon!

Best,
Chris
```
