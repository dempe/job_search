Mostly standard form fields, but there is a cover letter option, so here we go.

```
Hello,

First of all, thanks for taking the time to read my application.

I'm applying for Senior Backend Engineer, because I think I have the skills to really make an impact at Real. Java, microservices, and databases are right up my alley, so I should be able to hit the ground running.

## My Background

I started working in data ingestion in 2013 consuming data from a variety of sources (Twitter, Facebook, blogs, random forums, etc.) into a canonical comment model. I built a general purpose interface for consuming social media APIs and a web crawling framework all in Java. Data was stored in PostGres, HBase, ElasticSearch, and real time data came in from Kafka.

My next company was larger and had a lot more services running in a micorservices backend. I built an API gateway to provide a single interface for things like auth and rate limiting. I also got a lot of exposure to ElasticSearch and HBase.

At my next job, Octimine, I set up a Kafka for real time data ingestion, as their batch job ran weekly but data came in in real time. This imported data from the data warehouse to ElasticSearch while calculating a few analytics as well. I used Kafka Streams and everything was written in Java.

Most recently, I've started working on my own company, Dempewolf LLC, building educational mobile apps. This has given me a lot of hands-on experience, especially with AWS and frontend dev, which I had never touched before.  I use a combination of API Gateway, Lambda, and DynamoDB to verify purchase requests from the app.

## What's Next for Me

I've wanted to do my own thing for a while now. I did. I learned a lot. But now it's time to return to fulltime employment. I miss the camaraderie of working on a team in a fast-paced environment with decent autonomy. I do my best work in collaboration.

Moreover, at my solo-company, I'm in charge of everything - not just engineering (backend and frontend) but also marketing and sales, finances and accounting. I want to focus on what I do best - building resilient, scalable backend systems.

There's also the issue of growth. Being realistic, I'm unlikely to encounter whole categories of problems that I would at a larger company (things like coordinated deployments, millions of requests per second, etc.). I want to continue to grow as an engineer, and in order to do so, I need new problems to solve.

## Conclusion

Thanks for reading. I'd be thrilled to interview with Real, and I hope you'll consider me in your applicant search.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Best,
Chris Dempewolf
```
