## Interview Post-mortem

Just wanted to write about this because it's very hard to believe how ridiculous this was.

- They wanted me to work for free for four years (equity is imaginary money)
- All equity vests after 4 years
- Equity is given out, not based on time spent working for them, but via "milestones" that are determined by the CEO *NOT AHEAD OF TIME*.  In other words, the CEO gives out equity whenever he sees fit/feels like it
- They would tell me absolutely nothing about the product other than it's in React/Typescript, uses S3, and is something to do with media vault/streaming
- The whole product was "AI scaffolded" (i.e., vibe coded) by the CEO who knows nothing about software development
- They wanted me to sign a *very* restrictive NDA before even giving me a technical interview
- They kept deflecting all questions related to runway, like a good politician
- Every time they didn't want to answer a question, they just referred back to the NDA — "uhh, not sure I can answer that because of the NDA.  I don't know what I can and can't say.  I gotta refer back to that."
- The dev, Chris, was extremely informal. Like, I'm pretty informal, but this guy annoyed even me.  He was late, talking to other people without muting, kept walking around and moving, did the interview from his phone so he was at weird angles, was completely unprepared for nearly all of my questions, etc.  Exactly the type of guy I would expect to sign an NDA with Hector for imaginary money that just might liquidate someday, but is not even possible before 4 years, and even then, it's a long, very long shot.
- Who in their right mind would ever, ever sign such a deal?  How are you supposed to stay afloat for 4 years (at minimum and even then it's very likely you'll never see any money) with no income?
