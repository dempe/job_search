>  Please tell us about the time you, most successfully hacked some (non-computer) system to your advantage. *

```
I guess my most successful non-computer hacks have been mental/psychological.  Around my mid thirties, I started to really appreciate the value of consistency and habits.  I started building habits slowly, a few minutes a day.  I started stacking one habit after another, so that I have a nice stable daily routine.  With this new consistency, I've learned to juggle, read and listen to Japanese, use the Japanese abacus (soroban), etc.  It's really incredible how much just 10 minutes per day compounds over the years.
```
