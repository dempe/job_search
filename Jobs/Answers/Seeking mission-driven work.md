## Enveritas

Source: [[Jobs/Job Applications/Enveritas]]

> Why do you want to work with us? (A few brief sentences is fine.)

```
As compelling as the tech stack and company culture sound, the thing that most excites me about Enveritas is the actual company mission.

I worked as an English teacher in Mexico (Mexico City and Veracruz) for two years. Some of the students I taught were extremely poor. Actually helping them learn and motivate them was, by far, the most fulfilling experience of my life.

After going back to software engineering, I've missed having that sense of fulfillment in my work. Enveritas feels like an opportunity to get that back.
```
