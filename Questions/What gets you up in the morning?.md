I love learning. I think everyone has this innate desire to learn new things. This is why I travel, for example. Traveling to new countries and trying their foods, seeing their culture, etc, is, really, an intense learning experience.

That's also why I love tech. There's an endless amount of things to learn in tech. There's always something new. Good management, good coworkers, and a good code review system add to that. I love that, in tech, most companies are more results-driven and care less about degrees.
