Simplicity is number one for me. Something will always go wrong. We can't stop that, but we can limit that via simplicity. Simplicity means that fewer things can go wrong.

Simplicity also means that your code, what you make is easier for other to understand. Programs have two very distinct audiences - the computer and other people. I think most people forget about the later, and just tend to write something that runs.
